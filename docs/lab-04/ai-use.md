## 1. Which AI I used

**Claude Code** in the terminal, with access to the repository and to the `gh` command line
tool. **Claude Opus 5.5** ran the grill and wrote the plan, and verifies the work of the
others. **Claude Sonnet 5.5 sub-agents** implement: each is launched by the Opus 5.5 session
with a written brief and works alone in the repository. This engineering contract was
written by one of them.
